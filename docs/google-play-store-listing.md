# Google Play Store Listing

Use this as the source of truth for the default English (United States) Google

## App details

**App name** (11/30 characters)

> HousePoints

**Short description** (70/80 characters)

> Award points, celebrate achievements, and rally your house to the top.

**Full description**

> Bring friendly competition and recognition to your organization with
> HousePoints. Award points for positive contributions, celebrate achievements,
> and see how every house is progressing throughout the season.
>
> STAY UP TO DATE
>
> View your active season, house standings, member scores, and recent activity
> from one mobile dashboard. Switch between organizations when you belong to
> more than one.
>
> RECOGNIZE CONTRIBUTIONS
>
> Award points to members and record the reason for each contribution. React to
> activity to celebrate teammates and see who joined in.
>
> RECEIVE TIMELY UPDATES
>
> Review in-app notifications and, when enabled, receive push notifications for
> relevant HousePoints activity. Notification links take you directly to the
> related dashboard, activity, or invitation.
>
> MANAGE ON THE GO
>
> Authorized administrators and owners can manage member house assignments,
> update eligible roles, create invitation links, and access enabled point
> adjustment tools. Available actions depend on your organization role and
> settings.
>
> YOUR ORGANIZATION, YOUR HOUSE
>
> HousePoints keeps scores, activity, notifications, and administrative actions
> scoped to the active organization. Sign in securely to access the
> organizations you have joined.
>
> An internet connection and a HousePoints account with an organization
> membership are required. Some features depend on your organization’s
> configuration and your assigned role.

## App access notes for review

HousePoints requires authentication. Supply Google Play reviewers with a
dedicated, non-production-personal test account that belongs to a review-safe
organization. The account should have a house assignment and enough activity
to demonstrate the dashboard, leaderboard, activity, reactions, and
notifications.

If administrator features should be reviewed, provide a separate admin test
account or explain the role change. Never place credentials in this repository;
enter them only in Play Console's app-access instructions.
